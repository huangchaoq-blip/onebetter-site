Calm Creator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-22

This policy applies to Calm Creator, a teleprompter and recording app provided by Corevo. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in.

Your content stays on your device

Your scripts are stored locally on your device, in the app's own storage. Recordings are first written to a local folder on your device that is excluded from device backups. When a take finishes, it is imported into your Photos library, and the in-app copy is removed only after that import is confirmed. If an import fails, the take stays in the app so you can retry.

The app does not upload your scripts or recordings anywhere. Once a recording is in your Photos library, it is managed by iOS and your own Photos and iCloud settings, like any other video in your library.

Data collection

Calm Creator does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it does not use the advertising identifier or App Tracking Transparency. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Camera, microphone, and Photos

The camera and microphone are used only while you record. The app accesses your Photos library only to save finished recordings, as described above.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. Calm Creator does not receive or store your payment card details. The app uses purchase and restore information, verified on your device, to manage Pro access.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Privacy questions and requests
