TinyCompress Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21

This policy applies to TinyCompress, a photo compression app provided by Corevo.

Your photos

TinyCompress compresses photos on your device. We do not upload your photos to compress them. If you grant the required permission, the app saves compressed copies to your Photos library. The app stores its settings and information needed to manage compression and save retries on your device. Our custom analytics events do not include photo contents, filenames, local file paths, or EXIF metadata.

Product analytics

TinyCompress uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to understand how people use the app, improve reliability and features, and understand retention and Pro adoption. Analytics may include app launches, sessions, and screen views; the number of selected photos; the selected compression mode; batch-level file sizes and compression ratios; successful saves; visits to and interactions with the Pro offer; and purchase-related events. The analytics SDK may also collect device or app information and general location derived from the network connection. TinyCompress does not use GPS or photo metadata to determine your location for analytics.

Google Analytics uses an identifier for your app installation. Analytics activity can be associated with that installation over time, and the SDK may collect device-level identifiers where available. Those identifiers are not the same as your name or a TinyCompress account. TinyCompress does not require you to create an account to compress photos. See Privacy and Security in Firebase for information about Google's services.

How analytics data is used and shared

We use the analytics described above for product analysis. In our Google Analytics account, Google may use aggregated data for modeling and business insights, allow support representatives to access relevant data for troubleshooting, and use Analytics account information to provide business recommendations. The account's sharing setting for Google products and services is turned off. These are separate settings; turning one off does not disable the others.

Analytics retention

Our Google Analytics property is configured to retain event-level data for two months and user-level data for 14 months. New activity can restart the retention period for a user identifier. Some records, such as key events, are subject to separate Google Analytics retention rules, and standard aggregated reports are not subject to these time limits.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. TinyCompress does not receive or store your payment card details. The app uses purchase and restore information to manage Pro access. The analytics service may also measure purchase-related events separately from payment processing.

Privacy questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests about TinyCompress, email Corevo at onebetter.app4u@gmail.com. Please do not send photos, payment details, or other sensitive information with your request.
