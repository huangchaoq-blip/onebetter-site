Calm Creator Support

Calm Creator is a teleprompter and recording app for solo creators filming talking-head videos.

Read your script while you record — no subscription, no account, and mirror mode is included free. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited scripts, folders, batch import, shooting presets, and 4K recording on supported devices.

Your scripts and recordings stay on your device. Finished takes are saved to your Photos library.
